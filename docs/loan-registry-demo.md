# Loan Registry: the agent-bank walkthrough

Everything below runs in the browser, in the **Loan Registry** workspace, with no terminal. Judges sign in with any email; the agent-bank steps use the host's operator account and two clearly labelled automated institutions.

## The four steps

1. **Issue an asset** (`/issue`). Name the asset under the Meridian Holdings credit agreement, choose its facility type, principal, maturity and all-in rate, allocate the syndicate across the lenders on the register, and confirm. The wizard runs each Hedera transaction and shows its receipt: ATS security created, agent bank registered as KYC issuer and holder, settlement engine approved as transfer sender, then for every lender allowlist, KYC and issuance of its par, then the unallocated remainder to the agent bank, and finally the salted-hash commitment of the private rate notice on the HCS notices topic. Around 15 transactions, two to three minutes.
2. **Loan register** (`/register`). One credit agreement, every asset issued under it. The asset you just issued sits at the top, tagged "Just issued by you", next to the assets already on the register: the pre-issued Tranche A (MHTLB-A), the Revolving Credit Facility (MH-RCF, 60m) and the Delayed Draw Term Loan (MH-DDTL, 40m) from `ops/deployments/testnet.json`, and any tranche issued earlier from this wizard (Tranche B and Tranche C at the time of writing). The agent bank is always the platform's operator account; the signed-in institution appears as a syndicate lender on whatever it holds. Select an asset to see its lender register: par per lender, share of the asset, live from the ATS security, and each lender's interest for the latest period.
3. **Transfer requests** (`/assignments`). Click **Create a demo transfer request**: Bishopsgate (automated) posts a US$1m sell RFQ on the HCS market, Aldgate (automated) quotes 99.00, the quote is accepted, and the assignment lands in the queue awaiting the agent bank. Review the parties, par and price, then **Approve transfer request**. The instruction goes on the settlement engine, both institutions sign their approval with their own automated quorums, the second approval schedules the settlement on the Hedera Schedule Service, and the network executes it at the settlement time (about four minutes after the quote). The register updates by itself.
4. **Interest** (on the register and on `/lifecycle`). A payout skips any holder whose desk has not yet signed its mock-USD association (the reason is shown next to the holder). Once that desk's quorum signs the setup step on its Institution page, `npm run demo:payout -- --facility SYMBOL --catch-up` pays only the holders that were skipped and merges them into the period's payout receipt; nobody is paid twice. Every asset has a committed rate notice. For assets the CRE confidential workflow has already processed, the register shows the released amounts per lender and the payout receipts. For a brand-new asset it shows the agent bank's own estimate from the committed notice until the next CRE run recomputes it inside the enclave (`npm run demo:cre` from the repository root runs the simulation for every asset; `npm run demo:payout -- --facility SYMBOL` pays a period).

## What is real

Every asset is a separate ATS bond-type security on Hedera testnet with allowlist and internal KYC; every par figure is a token balance read through the RegisterSnapshot contract. The demo transfer is a real RFQ, quote and acceptance on the HCS topic, a real instruction on the settlement engine, real Privy intents signed by the automated institutions' key quorums, and a real Hedera scheduled transaction. Nothing signs a human desk wallet. Only the first tranche (MHTLB-A) is wired into the secondary market; the other assets are register-only in this demo.

## Host setup

- `ISSUANCE_DEMO_ENABLED=true` (default) and `ISSUANCE_DEMO_LIMIT` (default 20, max 50) bound the funded issuances per deployment. Each issuance costs the operator a few HBAR.
- `AUTOMATED_DESK_KEYS` and the two automated institutions (`aldgate` market maker, `bishopsgate` demo counterparty). Provision them once with `npx tsx scripts/provision-automated-desk.mts <id> "<Name>" [--demo-counterparty]` from `web/`. The first key is also the automated compliance co-signer on self-service judge desks (quorum: judge + co-signer + a per-desk reserve key, threshold 2), so a judge signs once per approval.
- `DESK_RESET_EMAILS`: a comma-separated email group whose members (plus-aliases included) see a **Reset desk** button in the header on self-service desks only. It drops that desk's record so the next page load provisions a fresh one; on-chain history is untouched. Named institutions (Meridian, Halcyon, the automated desks) can never be reset from the app. A plus-aliased sign-in gets a distinct desk name ("Adrija Judge2 Capital"), so test desks stay apart on the register; platform administrators can remove stale records from Administration.
- If a named institution's record is ever lost, `npx tsx scripts/recover-institution.mts meridian` rebuilds it from the wallet's Privy intents and Hedera receipts without signing anything.
- Any page load runs the market tick (onboarding, gas float for every desk wallet, automated signatures, settlement sync). The tick, the issuance wizard, agent consent and the admin actions all share one lock on the agent bank's signing account, so its transactions never race for a nonce. A caller that finds the lock busy waits for it (up to 30 seconds server-side; the wizard keeps retrying and shows "The agent bank is finishing another transaction").
